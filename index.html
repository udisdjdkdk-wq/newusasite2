Questions

Common questions

Are the recipes suitable for beginners?

Most recipes assume basic kitchen skills and explain any technique that goes beyond that. Look for the difficulty note near the top of each recipe.

Can I adjust serving sizes?

Yes. Quantities are listed per serving so you can scale up or down. Baking recipes scale more reliably when you keep the ratios exact rather than rounding.

What if I am missing an ingredient?

Many recipes note common substitutions. When a substitution changes texture or cooking time, the recipe says so.

How do I store leftovers?

Each recipe includes storage guidance where it matters, such as how long a dish keeps refrigerated and whether it freezes well.

What equipment do I need?

Standard home equipment covers nearly everything. When a recipe calls for something specific, such as a stand mixer or a Dutch oven, it is listed before the steps.
